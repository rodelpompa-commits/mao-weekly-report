Staff Reporting Reference

Reports should be informative and analytical, with current dates and a clear period covered.

For farm or field visits, include applicable technical details such as fertilizers or pesticides used, farming practices, variety planted, soil and land preparation, irrigation, pest and disease incidence, crop condition, yield estimate, constraints, best practices, post-harvest and marketing information.

The checklist grade applies only to technical assistance or field visit reports. The system detects the score from the written accomplishment and field evidence; staff cannot manually select checklist items.
